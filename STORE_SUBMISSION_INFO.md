## Short description

KY CHECKIN helps employees record attendance and access HR services from mobile.

## Full description draft

KY CHECKIN is a Digital HR mobile app for employees and administrators. It
supports attendance check-in/check-out, QR/NFC workflows, leave and payroll
views, notices, meetings, internal chat, and company HR services.

## Permission explanations

- Camera: scan server, attendance, and meeting QR codes; attach images where enabled.
- Location: validate attendance check-in and check-out based on workplace rules.
- Notifications: receive HR alerts, chat messages, app updates, and reminders.
- Microphone: record and send voice messages in chat.
- Photos/media: attach images or documents in supported workflows.
- NFC: register or scan NFC tags for attendance where enabled.
- Biometrics/Face ID: protect or confirm attendance actions where enabled.
